# Comparative research implications for Open Asset Lab

Research date: **2026-09-26**. The full corpus is in [MegaMod's research index](https://github.com/madpai/megamod-showdown/blob/main/docs/research/README.md); its [synthesis](https://github.com/madpai/megamod-showdown/blob/main/docs/research/CROSS_ENGINE_SYNTHESIS.md), [content registration](https://github.com/madpai/megamod-showdown/blob/main/docs/research/CONTENT_REGISTRATION.md), [package study](https://github.com/madpai/megamod-showdown/blob/main/docs/research/PACKAGE_MOD_FORMAT.md), and [recommendations](https://github.com/madpai/megamod-showdown/blob/main/docs/research/MEGAMOD_RECOMMENDATIONS.md) are the most directly relevant documents here. These are **research proposals**, not implemented importer/package features. Current behavior remains documented in [ARCHITECTURE.md](ARCHITECTURE.md), [RUNTIME_PACKAGE.md](RUNTIME_PACKAGE.md), and [ASSET_LAB_VISION.md](ASSET_LAB_VISION.md).

**N1/N2/X1 follow-up (2026-09-26):** MegaMod's [source-level boundary review](https://github.com/madpai/megamod-showdown/blob/main/docs/research/CURRENT_RUNTIME_CONTENT_BOUNDARY_REVIEW.md) records that OALMAP manifest spawn teams, flags, breakables and weather are runtime inputs; its `map_id` is not yet checked against the selected filename. The single canonical **research recommendation** for definition IDs is [`namespace:type/name`](https://github.com/madpai/megamod-showdown/blob/main/docs/research/CONTENT_ID_GRAMMAR_RECOMMENDATION.md), for example `source:map/de_dust2`. Namespace ownership, exact lowercase ASCII and explicit legacy mapping belong in OAL validation; this is not a current package field. The [world-event slice](https://github.com/madpai/megamod-showdown/blob/main/docs/research/WORLD_EVENT_SLICE_RECOMMENDATION.md) is likewise a proposed contract.

## Practical sequence

1. Finish N1 field/ordinal inventory and N2 **read-only** candidate-ID/collision audit. Do not migrate current packages during MegaMod's v10 compatibility fix.
2. For X1, extract the existing OAL compiler's normalized-world validation/write seam and pass a [programmatic original synthetic world](https://github.com/madpai/megamod-showdown/blob/main/docs/research/X1_ORIGINAL_AUTHORING_PATH.md) through it. Check placed IDs, links, cycles and limits. Do not write package bytes directly in the fixture.
3. Prove a second source family (original glTF) before broad Mesh/Material/Skeleton IRs; the X1 compiler seam gives it a target without making glTF a prerequisite for Button → Relay → Door.
4. Prototype a small immutable definition registry and then one host-authoritative scripted ability after X1 reveals actual fields and handles.
5. Delay package closure/lockfile, container redesign, cross-package patching and structural analysis until real content requires them.

**Publication boundary:** an importer being technically feasible says nothing about permission to redistribute its input or output. Keep private game data out of both repositories, as [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md) requires.
